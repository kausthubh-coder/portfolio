## Plan

Quick wins (one session):

1. **Repo hygiene.** Point `origin` at `kausthubh-coder/portfolio.git`, add `.gitattributes` (`* text=auto eol=lf`), pin dependencies to the installed versions, fast-forward the main checkout to `origin/main`. *Why:* Safety, Steering. *Check:* `git remote -v`, a clean `bun install`, `bun run build`. *Size:* XS.
2. **Verify skill.** Add `.agents/skills/verify-portfolio` and `scripts/shots.mjs`: build, preview on a free port, screenshot every route at 1280 and 390 in light and dark, fail on console errors or horizontal overflow. *Why:* Verification. *Check:* it runs green on the current site and fails on a planted overflow. *Size:* S.
3. **AGENTS.md** of about 30 lines: routes, where content lives, the commands, the verify skill, and the rules above (no title, keep the intro, no placeholders, plain copy). *Size:* XS.
4. **Fix the three PR #3 bugs** (findings 9–11) and the preload warning (12). *Size:* S.

Medium (one to two sessions):

5. **System tokens.** Type scale (6 sizes, 3 weights), spacing scale, colour roles, and a monochrome link style replacing the blue. Move components onto the tokens and delete orphans. *Why:* findings 2, 4, 5. *Check:* inventory again: font sizes 16 → 6 or fewer, weights 11 → 3, no hue outside logos. *Size:* M.
6. **Dark theme** designed on its own terms: `prefers-color-scheme` plus a small toggle (a micro-interaction moment). *Why:* finding 6. *Check:* verify skill dark shots. *Size:* S–M.
7. **Content-driven view transitions.** Replace the 12 hard-coded slug selectors with `view-transition-class: project-logo`, so any number of projects morph. *Why:* Steering, needed for six projects. *Check:* home ↔ each project morphs in Chrome; other browsers navigate normally. *Size:* S.
8. **Project content per file (M3).** Move to `src/content/projects/<slug>.md`, an Astro content collection with a zod schema, with images in `public/projects/<slug>/`. Drop the dead `facts` and `note` fields. Adding a project becomes one file plus images. *Why:* Locality, and finding "Content: dead fields". *Check:* build plus the verify skill; the schema rejects a missing field. *Size:* M.
9. **Content update** for all six. New copy (plain, lowercase), new logos (monochrome at rest, colour on hover), fresh screenshots per project, `site.description` and share image. *Size:* M–L, mostly capturing screenshots.

Revamp (the design work, after 5–9):

10. **Home as a scannable index.** Identity row and line of voice stay, and the intro stays. Projects become a list of rows (mark, name, one line, status, year) that reads in 3 seconds without hovering. Micro-interactions on top:
    - The row's logo turns from grey to colour on hover or focus.
    - A small screenshot peeks beside the cursor on desktop.
    - Rows have a press scale of 0.98.
    - `j`/`k` or arrow keys move through rows, and Enter opens.
    - A "copy email" morph confirms with "copied".
    - A live status dot shows on live projects.
    - Optionally, a local time line ("it's 7:40am for me").

    The logo pile and roll call are retired, or kept as the 404's toy. *Why:* findings 1, 7, 8. *Check:* verify skill at both widths; every project's name and one line readable with no interaction. *Size:* L.
11. **Project page diet.** Two screens of content: hero with status and links, a screenshot strip with captions, three features, one "built with" line. Architecture moves into a collapsible "how it works". *Why:* finding 3. *Check:* page height ≤ ~2,200 px at 1280. *Size:* M.
12. **Workflow page refresh.** Reuse the list-row component, monochrome icons, update the tools, bump the date. *Size:* S.

Suggested order: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 7 → 8 → 9 → 10 → 11 → 6 → 12. Each step ends with build plus the verify skill.
